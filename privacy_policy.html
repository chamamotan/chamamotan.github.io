Privacy Policy

How PLUMERIA Inc. handles personal information in all of the games and apps it publishes.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PLUMERIA Inc. (“we”) handles personal information in all games and apps we publish (the “Service”).

You can play without signing up: when you first launch a game, a random anonymous user ID (a guest account) is created automatically, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Some games (currently Come On Zombie) also let you sign in with your Google account; if you do, we receive that Google account’s email address and account ID.

Some features below (Google sign-in, data backup, server mail, etc.) are not offered in every game.

2. Retention and deletion

  • User ID, account information, game data backup, server mail: kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete them, or until the Service ends, then deleted without delay.
  • Purchase records: 5 years, as required by the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce.
  • Support records: 3 years, as required by the same Act.
  • Usage statistics: deleted after 14 months under our Google Analytics retention setting.
  • Advertising and error information: as set out in each provider’s policy (Google, Unity).

Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Your rights

  • You may ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. Email contact@plumeriagames.com with the game name; for a guest account, also include your user ID if the game’s settings screen shows one.
  • To delete your account and its data, follow the steps on our Account Deletion page. In short:
    • Signed in with Google: email us from that Google account’s email address with the subject [Game Name] Account Deletion Request.
    • Guest account: use the account deletion option in the game’s settings screen.
  • We act on your request within 10 days of receiving it and reply to let you know the result.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your user ID, account information, game data backup and server mail. Purchase records that the law requires us to keep are stored separately for the period in section 2 and then deleted. Usage statistics are not linked to your account and expire as set out in section 2.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the game data on your device, but not the data on our servers. For a guest account, delete it in the game before uninstalling: once the app is removed, we cannot tell which guest account was yours.
  • A legal guardian of a child under 14 may make the same requests.

6. Automatic collection

The apps do not use web cookies. They use the Firebase app instance ID and your device’s advertising ID for usage statistics and ads. See section 4 for how to opt out.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that such information has been processed, we delete it without delay.

8. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Database access rules that limit each player to their own data; administrative access limited to as few people as possible
  • Tamper checks on game data stored on the device
